# Final Product Workflow

The final product phase starts only after the researcher accepts the latest loop artifact.

## Product Selection Contract

Every product-selection prompt must provide one recommended option, concrete options for paper/report/software/multi-track output, and a free-form natural-language field for goals, constraints, style, audience, venue, release expectations, or other requirements.

## Track Defaults

- Paper defaults to English LaTeX/PDF and requires evidence-linked claims.
- Research report defaults to process-rich output with HTML, LaTeX/PDF, and PPT paths available.
- Software defaults to stable, engineered, polished, user-friendly output with documentation and tests.

## Gate Rules

Public export, paper submission, software release, external upload, and external writeback always require explicit human approval.
