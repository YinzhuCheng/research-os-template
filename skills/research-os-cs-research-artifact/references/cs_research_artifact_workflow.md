# CS Research Artifact Workflow

Route by primary contribution:

- Theory or algorithms: problem spec, invariants, complexity, proof obligations, lower/upper-bound context.
- Systems: architecture, workload, environment, baselines, observability, replay, resource envelope.
- Software engineering: task, artifact corpus, human factors, evaluation protocol, validity threats.
- Security: threat model, assets, adversaries, disclosure boundary, mitigation assumptions.
- HCI: user population, tasks, ethics, study protocol, measures, accessibility, and limitations.
- Databases or PL: semantics, workloads, correctness, benchmarks, and compatibility.

Acceptance rule: a CS artifact claim requires problem spec, correctness or benchmark route, environment/artifact inventory, and residual risk notes.
