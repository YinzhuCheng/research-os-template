# Research OS Evidence

Use this skill when research objects need evidence links.

## Workflow

1. Read `PUBLIC/claim_evidence_matrix.yaml` and the latest research brief.
2. Assign or reuse IDs for `RQ-*`, `HYP-*`, `CLAIM-*`, `EXP-*`, `RUN-*`, and `RES-*`.
3. Link each claim to evidence and counterevidence.
4. Mark unsupported claims as `draft`, `partially_supported`, `unsupported`, or `retired`.
5. Never let the paper assert a claim that has no evidence link.

## Quick Checks

- Read `references/evidence_rules.md` before editing evidence records.
- Prefer evidence from experiments, source papers, and reproducible logs over free-form memory.
