# Evidence Rules

## Claim Status

- `draft`: plausible but not yet tested.
- `supported`: backed by enough evidence for the current venue target.
- `partially_supported`: evidence supports a narrower or weaker claim.
- `unsupported`: current evidence does not support the claim.
- `retired`: claim should not be used in paper text.

## Minimum Claim Requirements

Every public claim needs:

- evidence link,
- counterevidence check,
- target dissemination artifact,
- privacy level,
- limitation if the claim is not fully supported.
