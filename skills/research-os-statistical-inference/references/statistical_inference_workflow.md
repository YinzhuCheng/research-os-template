# Statistical Inference Workflow

Default loop:

1. Define the estimand before methods.
2. Record design and identification assumptions.
3. Plan sampling, power or precision, multiplicity, and missing data handling.
4. Fit or plan models only after the analysis target is explicit.
5. Record diagnostics, robustness, and sensitivity analyses.
6. Communicate uncertainty with effect sizes, intervals, posterior summaries, and limitations.
7. Check ethical practice, privacy, subgroup risks, and disclosure boundaries.

Acceptance rule: a statistical conclusion requires estimand, identification or design assumptions, diagnostics, uncertainty, sensitivity, and ethics notes.
