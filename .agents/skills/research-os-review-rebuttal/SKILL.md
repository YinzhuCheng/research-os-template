# Research OS Review Rebuttal

Use this skill after a paper draft exists, or after a formal report draft explicitly requests review/rebuttal-style iteration.

## Workflow

1. Read paper or formal report draft, appendix or supplemental material, claim-evidence matrix, result analysis, and target venue or report audience.
2. Generate multiple reviewer roles:
   - novelty skeptic,
   - method correctness reviewer,
   - experiment reviewer,
   - writing and presentation reviewer,
   - reproducibility reviewer.
3. For every concern, assign severity and required action.
4. Draft rebuttal only for defensible points; otherwise create revision tasks.
5. Update claim-evidence and work orders for any needed experiments.

## Quick Checks

- Read `references/review_protocol.md` before generating reviews.
- Do not hide serious weaknesses; turn them into revisions or limitations.
