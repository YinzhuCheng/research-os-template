# Review Protocol

## Severity

- `S1`: paper-blocking correctness or evidence problem.
- `S2`: major weakness likely to reduce acceptance chance.
- `S3`: local clarity, missing detail, or presentation issue.
- `S4`: minor wording or formatting issue.

## Output

- Review summary.
- Strengths.
- Weaknesses.
- Questions.
- Rebuttal matrix.
- Revision work orders.
