# Feasibility Probe Rules

- A probe is decision-oriented, not publication-oriented.
- The budget is researcher-defined; templates must not supply an amount.
- Compare against the smallest meaningful criterion for the domain. This may be a physical measurement, expert judgment, logical consistency, user response, reproduced observation, cost threshold, or baseline only when the field requires it.
- Record three outcomes: success, failure, and unclear.
- If the probe cannot be designed without a missing resource decision, route to alignment.
