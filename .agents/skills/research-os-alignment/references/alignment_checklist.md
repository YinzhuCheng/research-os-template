# Alignment Checklist

## Required Sections

- Current understanding.
- Intended audience.
- Success criteria.
- In scope.
- Out of scope.
- Known constraints.
- Unresolved decisions.
- Recommended defaults.
- Stage-gate decision.

## Question Priority

1. Questions that change the research goal.
2. Questions that change privacy or public release.
3. Questions that change experimental validity.
4. Questions that change budget or time.
5. Questions that change paper positioning.

## Decision Record

Every confirmed decision should become `DEC-*` and link to affected work orders, claims, experiments, or paper sections.
